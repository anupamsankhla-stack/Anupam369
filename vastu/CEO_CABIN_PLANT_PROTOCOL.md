# CEO / MD cabin threshold — Vastu + Feng Shui plant protocol

**Decision:** Install two living medium plants immediately outside the cabin door.
**Primary pair:** Money Plant on moss-pole + Areca Palm.
**Menu (do not install all five):** Peace Lily, Lucky Bamboo (5 or 8 stalks), Jade Plant.
**Bench:** Snake Plant (door guard only), Rubber Plant, ZZ Plant.

## Hard rules
- Living plants only. No silk, no cactus, no dying leaves.
- Keep 90 cm door swing / fire path clear. Nothing on the centreline.
- Prefer North / East / North-East / South-East walls. Avoid water plants in South-West.
- Height cap: plant + pot ≤ 140 cm.
- Maximum two plants at one door unless corridor > 2.1 m and faces a lift or sharp beam.

## Stalk code (Lucky Bamboo)
- 3 = happiness / start
- 5 = wealth
- 8 = prosperity (preferred for business)
- Avoid 4

## Care
- Monday water ritual (PA).
- Lucky Bamboo: filtered water weekly.
- Replace yellowing stock within 48 hours.
